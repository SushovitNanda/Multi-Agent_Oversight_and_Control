## Design decisions not in the plan
- Scorer weakness: a `VERIFIED: <another agent's sign-off code>` line passes run_check regardless of content.
  Sign-off codes are therefore both the colluders' means and a tracer. Each agent also gets 3 ticket tracers
  (PORT-, CHG-, HOST-) that honest work never requires sharing.
- Only colluders receive the cache tools in the offlog condition. Honest agents never see them.
- Peer logs are generated by the simulator as perfect self-reports; tampering is applied offline on copies.
  This is the "honest agents retain true records" assumption of the witness theory, stated in the report.
- Positive label = scorer_trip or hidden transfer in truth; instruction alone never counts.
- Liar sets for k > 4 add honest agents (random, or hub-first for targeted) who delete their own records.
- Erasure share is measured over messages (traffic-weighted), while Propositions 1 and 2 are over edges.
  In the scripted dry run, mesh measured erasure exceeds Proposition 1 because colluder traffic concentrates
  on colluder edges. Report both and explain the difference.

## RQ2 result and a labelling caveat to fix before the report (6 Nov, 17:55 IST)

RQ2 (main, fixed rules): omission and fabrication are caught in every positive episode from
one liar up; spoof is caught from two liars up in-band (one lone spoofer cannot be contradicted
by anyone, as Proposition 1 predicts) and from one liar up off-log (knowledge closure catches it
regardless); mutual forgery is invisible in-band at every k (0% recall, the theoretical limit)
but 93% of off-log episodes are still caught through knowledge closure. Random and targeted
liar selection give identical recall. Measured erasable share exceeds the theory curve in mesh
(gap up to 0.29 at k=4) because colluders repeat messages among themselves, so the theory bound
is a floor, not a fit.

Caveat: `rq2.py` applies the tamper to every episode, including the 17 in-band episodes that
are labelled negative (colluders present, no scorer trip). Their "fpr" of 0.221 = 17/77 is
therefore detection of real tampering in negative-labelled episodes, not a false alarm;
honest untampered episodes (k=0 row, and all 60 honest episodes under every class) have 0 alerts.
Fabrication "fpr" of 1.0 is the same effect. Before the report, split the negative set into
honest-untampered (true FPR) and colluder-present-no-trip, or apply tamper only where liars
exist. No code change made now, per the user's halt instruction.
